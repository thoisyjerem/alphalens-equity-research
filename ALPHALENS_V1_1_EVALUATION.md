## 1. Executive assessment

V1.1 adds evidence-bound investment question framing and a separate analytical review to the existing application. Python still owns amounts, fiscal periods, calculations, source metadata and assembly. The original Gemini response and its historical mechanical verdict remain unchanged.

The improvement is a more useful, auditable research process: three distinct Microsoft numerical developments, competing mechanisms with evidence discriminators, accounting-attribution warnings and a second-company SEC validation. It is **not evidence that the revised Gemini prompt generates better live research**. No Gemini request was made; the last revised-prompt request remains HTTP 503 without an output.

**GO for a supervised JHI portfolio demonstration of the workflow; NO-GO for autonomous investment research or a decision-ready recommendation.** The final full suite passed **942 tests**, and the actual offline browser workflow and exports passed. The original model note remains insufficient for an investment decision; its qualitative limitations are not concealed by the new framework.

## 3. Investment reasoning improvements

Each selected finding retains its numerical observation, exact comparisons, fiscal dates and financial IDs. A separate reviewer framework supplies economic significance, an investment implication, constructive/cautionary mechanisms, conditions favouring each, and a specific unresolved question. Financial support establishes the observation, **not the proposed cause**. Missing or non-comparable inputs cause abstention; fewer than three findings are shown rather than inventing material developments.

For the actual Microsoft recording the selected developments are:

1. `2026:cash_flow_divergence`: OCF increased but cash PP&E increased more, reducing cash retained after defined reinvestment. The discriminator is productive deployment followed by cash contribution versus persistently elevated reinvestment or reversible cash timing.
2. `2026:revenue_profit_growth`: reported revenue and operating income increased. The discriminator is recurring operating progress versus currency, mix, cost timing or scope effects; the totals do not identify the cause.
3. `2026:earnings_conversion_tension`: net income improved while FCF/net income weakened. A net-income-to-OCF reconciliation followed by cash PP&E and lease investment is needed before using earnings growth as a proxy for distributable cash.

These are **three Python/reviewer developments, not three new Gemini findings**. The genuine model recording still contains two findings. The existing consolidated/cloud margin comparison supplies contrary context without pretending to prove its cause.

The first prompt integration failed an existing compact-context regression: 24,258 JSON characters exceeded the existing 20,000-character guard. The correction retained rich display records locally and sent only observation IDs, two hypothesis mechanisms, a question and evidence gap: **18,135 characters** for the tested offline packet. This is a character measurement, not live API token usage or proof of prompt quality. The old limit was preserved.

## 4. Analytical evidence checks

| Category | Rule and boundary |
|---|---|
| Verified observation | A small grammar of annual financial-direction statements matches checked cited facts and comparisons. This does not certify broader prose or completeness. |
| Deterministic calculation | Applicable source-bound calculations and relationships; exact financial definitions and periods remain inspectable. |
| Supported interpretation | Only a narrowly matched management cash-flow driver clause with matching annual financial direction. It verifies correspondence to a disclosed explanation, not its persistence or independent economic truth. |
| Unverified hypothesis | Conditional mechanisms, unanswered questions, unestablished causes or prose outside the bounded checks. An unsupported premise inside a question is flagged. |
| Unsupported claim | Identifiable structural, amount, period, source or accounting-attribution failure. Original words remain available as diagnostic/history, not promoted to verified analysis. |

Rules flag customer receipts/tax payments being treated as proof of working-capital improvement; non-recurring operating-profit explanations based only on net-income/EPS investment gains; company cash PP&E being relabelled AI-specific; and unsupported future-return claims. They reuse financial/quotation/comparative checks and reject some compound-direction reversals. Missing evidence and unrelated citations cannot automatically confer support. A bounded current-year causal passage check prevents an older-period quotation from inheriting current-year support.

The interface displays the claim, cited IDs, support basis and qualifications. Canonical historical `validate_analysis` results are not overwritten. Conditional bull/bear mechanisms remain hypotheses and must identify observation basis, mechanism and strengthening/weakening evidence. No additional AI validator or model request is used. The rules are conservative and incomplete: negation, complex sentences, omitted context and economic causality still require human review.

## 8. Remaining weaknesses and readiness

1. The genuine Gemini note remains shallow on forward cash economics and contains flagged support gaps. The revised prompt has no successful live quality evaluation. V1.1 improves process and questions, not demonstrated live model quality.
2. Deterministic support checks cover selected grammars/accounting errors, not general entailment. Even the Supported interpretation category requires an analyst to judge meaning, completeness and persistence.
3. Cash-tax/working-capital normalization, investment scope, segment economics, incremental returns and market expectations remain incomplete. No valuation, forecast, recommendation or AI-specific ROIC is produced.
4. Visa confirms partial deterministic generalisation, with important N/A values and no live Visa AI analysis. The existing focused-generation gate still needs sufficient distinct comparable observations and filing evidence; a one-signal Visa case is not claimed ready for that AI route.
5. Microsoft segment parsing remains deliberately limited to the verified retained table layout. Historical recordings and caches are dated, not continuously current evidence. Challenge the Thesis and optional OpenAI retain their prior live-testing limitations.

**Recommendation: GO for a supervised JHI portfolio demonstration**, with limitations explained. Demonstrate the financial reconciliation, specific competing questions, actual flagged model weakness and source inspection. Do not present this as a standalone investment report for circulation, general semantic verification or a successfully live-validated V1.1 model briefing. The detailed note/audit remains longer than the two-minute narrative; use selective navigation rather than reading every qualification aloud.
